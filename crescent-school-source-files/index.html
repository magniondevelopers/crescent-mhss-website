Welcome to Crescent

A tradition of learning since 1999

Crescent began on 2 June 1999 with 100 children in Pre-KG and LKG, founded by community members who wanted quality English-medium education within reach of every family.

Today the school serves students through Higher Secondary, with a disciplined environment where academics, values and character grow together.

  • Accessible education with a community-centred mission
  • Arabic from the basic level and value-based learning
  • Standard X approved in 2009, XI in 2011–12, XII in 2012–13
